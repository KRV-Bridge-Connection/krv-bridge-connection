# Privacy Policy for KRV Bridge Connection

At KRV Bridge Connection, we respect your privacy and believe in keeping things transparent. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our nonprofit resource center website, written in plain language.

## 1. How We Use (and Don't Use) Cookies
**We do not use cookies to track or identify you.** 

Our website only uses two strictly necessary cookies to keep the site functioning:
*   **Directory Sync:** A technical cookie that records the last time our resources directory database was updated so you have the latest information.
*   **Staff Access:** A secure token used exclusively to authenticate logged-in staff members or partners.

No other cookies are generated or used by our website itself.

## 2. Anonymous Site Analytics
To help us maintain and improve our services, we measure general site usage. This includes basic interactions like page loads, navigation through the site, and clicks on "call" or "email" buttons. 

**All analytics data is completely anonymous.** 
*   We do not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information.
*   We do not use tracking IDs to link your actions together. For example, our system logs that a call button was clicked in isolation, but it does not know who clicked it or what other pages that person visited.

## 3. Contact Form Data
Our primary goal is to connect you with the right assistance. If you submit information through our contact form, we may share those details with a relevant partner agency within our network. This is done exclusively to fulfill your request and get you the help you are seeking.

## 4. Embedded Content and Third-Party Services
Our site includes embedded features from external platforms, such as YouTube (which runs in privacy-enhanced "nocookie" mode) and DonorBox for contributions. 

Please note that these third-party services operate independently of our site. They may use their own cookies or collect data according to their own policies. Those external resources and platforms are not governed by this privacy policy.
